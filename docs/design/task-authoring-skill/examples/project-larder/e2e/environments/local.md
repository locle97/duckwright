## Seeded data

## Known quirks

- The basket badge in the header updates about a second after "Add to basket"; check the Basket page, not the badge.
- "Apply" for a promo code shows its message under the field, not as a toast.
- The card form is in an iframe (refs start with `f`).

## Off-limits

- Do not change anything under /admin/users. Do not use "Delete account". No refunds.
- Do not open the Stripe dashboard link.
